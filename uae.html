    Questions? Answers.

    The short version of how sending works.

    How do I send money?

    Choose where and how much, add your recipient and pay, then confirm and track it until it’s delivered.

    How will my recipient get the money?

    Straight to their bank account, or as cash collected at a nearby pickup point.

    How can I pay?

    Pay in the LuLu Money app, or settle at a LuLu Money counter.

    What fee will I pay?

    The fee is shown up front, together with today’s rate, before you send. No hidden charges.

    How long does a transfer take?

    Transfers are delivered in minutes. Timing depends on the destination and the payout method.

    Is there an offer on my first transfer?

    Yes. Enjoy a welcome benefit on your first money transfer.
